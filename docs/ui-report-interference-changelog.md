## A. Hybrid Athlete Report — rebuilt from every data source the app has

**Was:** four stored fields (score trend, readiness trend, one interference sentence, a 5K
target), generated by a monthly cron, blank until the 1st of the next month.

**Now:** built live on every visit from the trailing 30 days. Nine sections:

1. **Where you stand** — Split Index (with tier), Endurance, Strength; each with its change over the period; best-ever index and date; which side is lower.
2. **What a coach would say first** — up to six generated sentences (index movement, balance, consistency, lift trends, 5K prediction, recovery, load spikes, interference costs).
3. **How your training split** — strength/endurance bar by time; sessions, hours, distance, load per side; per-sport table (sessions, time, distance, average score).
4. **Consistency** — sessions/week vs target, weeks on target, current streak, longest break, a four-bar weekly chart.
5. **Strength** — best-ever squat/bench/deadlift and total; DOTS and IPF GL (or a Premium note); every lift with current vs best and trend, collapsed by default.
6. **Endurance** — predicted benchmark per sport (5K, 2K row, …); predicted race times 1500 m → marathon; actual race records; estimated VO₂max and lactate threshold.
7. **Recovery and load** — composed recovery score and band, readiness, this week's load vs 4-week average, injury zone (only with Article 9 consent; otherwise says how to turn it on), HRV.
8. **How your two sides affect each other** — both interference verdicts with sentence and advice, linking to the full page.
9. **Records set in the period.**

## C. Interference page — a verdict, not a chart

**Was:** a primer, then two cards each with a big percentage, a generated sentence, and a bar chart.

**Now:** each direction is one card that leads with a **verdict pill** — No cost / Small cost /
Real cost / Helps / Still learning — then the number, one plain sentence, one line of **what to
do**, and how many sessions it rests on (as dots against the minimum). The main visual needs no
axis: a row of four **day chips** (That day / Next day / 2 days / 3 days) coloured by verdict for
"how long does the effect last", and **two bars** (lighter vs heavier cardio week) for the other
direction. The recharts figures are kept, unchanged, under a closed **"See the detailed charts"**
section. The primer is one sentence with the 3-step mechanism under "How this is worked out".

Thresholds: under 3% is "no cost" (the engine's own line); 8% or more is "real cost".
