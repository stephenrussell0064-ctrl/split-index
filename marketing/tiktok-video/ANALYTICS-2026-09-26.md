## What it says

1. **The showcase post won, by a distance.** The one post framed as "data
   analytics on all your hybrid performance" did 2.3× the views of anything
   else, 10× the likes, and brought 15 of the account's new followers.
   Traffic was 88 % For You; its only search query was "split index".
2. **The "Who's the better hybrid athlete?" batch hit a ceiling.** Nine
   near-identical posts all landed between 707 and 731 views — that is
   TikTok's initial test distribution, and none of them earned a second
   push. The retention panel says "most viewers stopped watching at 0:01":
   the hook was losing people in the first second.
3. **The Riegel / 1RM prediction post retained best.** Fewest likes, but a
   4.65 s average watch and 6.1 % watched to the end, three times any other
   post. Concrete app features hold attention; a rhetorical question does not.
4. **Repetition is not testing.** Nine posts with the same caption and the
   same first second tell you one thing nine times. Change the hook, the
   first frame and the caption between variants, or post one and read it.

## What to do with the new direction

- Showcase videos: lead with a real screen and a real number in frame 1
  (the 75.9 dashboard, the predicted race times, the 1RM list). The
  real-footage film `out/real/` and the formats already do this.
- Personal videos: Stephen training, with the app's scoring of that exact
  session cut in. The ad is the score, not a slogan.
- Judge on average watch time and watched-full, not views, until a post
  clears the ~700 test batch. The 1.7K post is the only one that has.
- Post public, and leave it public. Check the Private setting first.
